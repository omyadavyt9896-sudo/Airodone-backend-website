Under the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) skill education directives aligned with NEP 2020, affiliated schools are encouraged to establish a dedicated Composite Skill Lab. Unlike single-subject science laboratories, a Composite Skill Lab provides a shared, cross-disciplinary space where students engage in vocational and vocational-exploratory modules from Classes VI to XII.

AIRODRONE designs, equips, and delivers complete turnkey setups that allow institutions to introduce practical problem-solving, rapid prototyping, and digital literacy without requiring multiple separate rooms or prohibitive capital expense.

Spatial Footprint

400 – 600+ Sq. Ft.

CBSE suggests a minimum floor space of 400 sq. ft. (for 20 concurrent students) or 600+ sq. ft. (for larger cohorts), divided into an ideation zone, an electronics bench, and a fabrication area.

20–30 Student Sizing

Electrical & Earthing

Dedicated Points & Safety

Proper 3-pin earthed power strips at each workbench, stabilized voltage supply, safety MCB switches, CO2/dry-powder fire extinguishers, and anti-static ESD-safe desktop mats.

ESD & MCB Protected

Modular Storage

Organized Tool Management

Heavy-duty lockable metal cupboards, component bin organizer cabinets, transparent labeled component totes, and pegboards for quick access and inventory audit compliance.

Audit Ready Inventory

Multi-Activity Zoning

Reconfigurable Pods

Hexagonal or mobile rectangular student workstations with castor locks, allowing smooth rearrangement from group presentations to individual mechanical fabrication sprints.
